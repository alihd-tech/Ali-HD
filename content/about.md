I'm Ali Heydari — a full-stack engineer who thrives at the intersection of performance, architecture, and user experience.

## Philosophy

I believe great software is built at the meeting point of three disciplines:

- **Performance engineering** — Systems that stay fast under real load, not just in benchmarks
- **Thoughtful architecture** — Patterns that make complexity manageable, not just tolerable
- **User experience** — Interfaces that respect people's time and attention

## What I Do

I design and build full-stack systems — from database schema to pixel-perfect UI. My work spans backend services in Scala and FastAPI, rapid application development with Laravel, and modern frontends with Nuxt, Vue, and TypeScript.

## Approach

I focus on the feedback loops that make products resilient under real-world conditions. Whether it's designing event-driven architectures, optimizing database query plans, or building CI/CD pipelines that teams actually trust — I care about the craft of writing code that others can reason about and systems that grow gracefully.

## Beyond Code

When I'm not shipping code, I'm thinking about how to make the next thing better — faster, simpler, more maintainable. I'm a lifelong learner and a firm believer that the best engineering comes from understanding the problem deeply before reaching for a solution.
